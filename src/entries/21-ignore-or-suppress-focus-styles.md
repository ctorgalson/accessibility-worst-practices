<section>

### Remediation / Prevention
- provide focus styles *and* hover styles
- specify a style [satisfying WCAG 1.4.11](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Understanding/non-text-contrast.html) for every possible control/background permutation
- consider how a given style may overlap with adjacent elements
</section>
<section>

### Do not
- use identical hover and focus styles
- specify invisible focus styles
