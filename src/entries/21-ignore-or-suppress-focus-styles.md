<section>

### Remediation / Prevention
- provide consistent focus styles
- ensure focus styles [satisfy WCAG 1.4.11](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Understanding/non-text-contrast.html) for every element/background permutation
- consider how focus indicators [may overlap with adjacent elements](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-not-obscured-minimum.html)
</section>
<section>

### Do not
- use identical styles for focus and other states
- ask for focus styles to be removed
- rely on colour alone to indicate focus

</section>
